## Description
Simply Remote turns your iPhone into a remote for the smart TV on your Wi-Fi. Open the app and it finds your TV by itself — no IR blaster, no account, no cloud.

WORKS WITH
• Samsung smart TVs (2016 and later)
• LG webOS TVs
• Roku players and Roku TVs (TCL, Hisense and more)
• Google TV and Android TV, including Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips and Chromecast with Google TV
• Sony Bravia
• Vizio SmartCast
• Amazon Fire TV and Fire TV Edition TVs
• Panasonic VIERA (2011–2018)

EVERYTHING A REMOTE DOES
• Arrow pad or swipe pad, OK, back and home
• Volume, mute and channels
• Power on and off
• Switch inputs: TV, HDMI and the TV's input list
• Open the TV's settings menu
• Type searches with your iPhone keyboard

ONE TAP TO YOUR APPS
Jump straight to Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max, Peacock, Apple TV or Paramount+ on the TV. Apps that aren't installed on your TV are hidden automatically.

WHAT TO WATCH
See what's trending and popular right now, find out which service is streaming it, and open it on your TV with one tap.

MORE WAYS TO CONTROL
• Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Watch Netflix with Simply Remote TV", "Mute the TV with Simply Remote TV"
• Apple Watch remote
• Four themes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Simply Remote talks only to the TVs on your own network. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.

Some TVs need a one-time step: choose Allow on the TV (Samsung, LG, Fire TV) or enter the code shown on screen (Google TV, Vizio). Fire TV needs ADB debugging turned on in Developer options. Sony Bravia needs a Pre-Shared Key set under IP control.

Movie and show data from TMDB. This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. Streaming availability by JustWatch. Simply Remote is not affiliated with any TV maker or streaming service; all trademarks belong to their owners.

## App Review notes
Simply Remote controls smart TVs over the local Wi-Fi network, so a compatible TV on the same network is needed to try the remote buttons. No account or login is required.

Without a TV you can still review:
• The home page and Settings (themes, reorder and hide streaming app shortcuts).
• Remote screen → Trending Now → See all: the What to Watch page and title details, with streaming availability (TMDB/JustWatch data).
• The TV picker (tap the TV name at the top of the remote): it searches the local network for supported TVs.

Movie and show data: the What to Watch section uses The Movie Database (TMDB) API under TMDB's free API terms. Simply Remote is free, with no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions, and earns no revenue from TMDB content, so no commercial agreement is required. As TMDB's terms require, the app shows the TMDB logo (smaller than our own branding) and the notice "This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB." in Settings → About & Credits and on the What to Watch page, and the TMDB logo next to Trending Now. Posters and artwork are served from TMDB's image API under those terms and belong to their owners. Streaming availability is JustWatch data provided through TMDB and is credited to JustWatch in the app.

The Local Network permission is used only to find and control TVs. The app was tested on a Samsung UN65RU7100 (2019). Streaming services appear as plain text buttons; the only third-party logo shown is TMDB's, as their API terms require.
