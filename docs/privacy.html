Simply Remote – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

What the app does

Simply Remote controls TVs on your own Wi-Fi network. It finds TVs on that network and sends them remote-control commands directly.

Data we collect

None. Simply Remote has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server that receives your data.

Data kept on your device

The app stores the list of TVs it found, their pairing keys and your settings on your iPhone only. Deleting the app removes them.

Network access

Local network access is used only to find and control your TVs. When the trending movies section is shown, the app requests public movie and show listings from The Movie Database (TMDB). That request includes no personal information. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability data is provided by JustWatch.

Apple Watch, Siri and widgets

These features pass button presses between your own devices and the app. Nothing leaves your devices except the commands sent to your TV.
